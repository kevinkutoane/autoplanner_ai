## 1. The 3-Step Daily Habit (Quick Start)

The easiest way to get the most value out of AutoPlanner is a simple, repeatable morning and evening routine:

```
┌────────────────────────┐      ┌────────────────────────┐      ┌────────────────────────┐
│   1. Brain Dump        │ ──▶  │   2. Review & Start    │ ──▶  │   3. Focus & Complete  │
│ Speak or type thoughts │      │ View smart timeline    │      │ Deep work with timer   │
│ Morning (2-3 minutes)  │      │ Adjust tasks as needed │      │ Celebrate milestones   │
└────────────────────────┘      └────────────────────────┘      └────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Morning Brain Dump (2 minutes)**: Open the app when you wake up or start work. Type or speak everything on your plate into the Brain Dump tab.
2. **Review Your Timeline (1 minute)**: AutoPlanner arranges your tasks into realistic time slots between your fixed calendar meetings and protects your energy peak hours.
3. **Focus & Check Off**: Launch the Focus Hub when it's time to work. As you complete tasks, earn streaks and track your progress throughout the day.

## 2. Brain Dump Studio: Capturing Your Thoughts

Never let a great idea or urgent task slip through the cracks.

### How to Use Brain Dump
- **Text Input**: Type out quick thoughts, errands, or project deliverables in plain language (e.g., *"Finish Q3 financial report by 2pm, email Sarah about designs, pick up groceries"*).
- **Voice Notes**: Tap the **Microphone** button to speak your thoughts aloud. AutoPlanner transcribes your voice directly into your notes.
- **Smart Task Conversion**: Tap **Organize with AI** to convert your raw notes into structured tasks with:
  - Estimated duration (15m, 30m, 60m, etc.)
  - Priority level (High, Medium, Low)
  - Required energy level (High focus, Medium, Quick win)
  - Target project or goal tag

> **Tip**: Don't worry about formatting or organizing while brain dumping. Just get everything out of your head; the app organizes it for you!

## 3. The Daily Planner: Mastering Your Schedule

The **Planner** tab is your command center for the day.

### Understanding Your Timeline
- **Fixed Meetings (Anchors)**: Any event imported from Google Calendar or created with a locked time has a lock icon. AutoPlanner will never double-book or overwrite these slots.
- **Flexible Tasks**: AutoPlanner intelligently fits your flexible tasks into free gaps in your day, matching high-focus tasks with your personal peak energy hours.
- **Capacity Meter**: At the top of your planner, a visual capacity bar shows your planned work hours vs. your healthy daily limit. If the bar turns amber or red, you may be overcommitting — consider moving low-priority tasks to tomorrow.

### Moving & Editing Tasks
- **Tap any task** to view details, mark it complete, edit duration, or change priority.
- **Drag & Drop**: Press and hold any task block on the timeline to move it to a different time slot.

### Automatic Schedule Catch-Up (Schedule Drift Recovery)
Life happens! If a meeting runs 30 minutes over, or your lunch break took longer than expected:
1. Tap the **Schedule Catch-Up** button on your Planner screen.
2. AutoPlanner will automatically slide your remaining flexible tasks forward into open afternoon slots, preserving your breaks and ensuring you finish without stress.

## 4. Focus Hub & Ambient Soundscapes

When it is time to work, the **Focus Hub** eliminates distractions and gets you into a flow state.

### Using the Focus Timer
- **Pomodoro Mode**: Work in concentrated 25-minute sprints followed by a relaxing 5-minute break.
- **Deep Work Mode**: Set longer 50-minute or 90-minute blocks for challenging, creative, or analytical tasks.
- **One-Tap Task Linking**: Pick the task you are currently working on so your logged focus minutes automatically count toward that task's completion.

### Ambient Soundscapes
Tap the **Audio** icon in the Focus Hub to enable soothing background audio:
- **Gentle Rain**: Calming rainfall to mask background noise.
- **Forest Birds**: Natural woodland ambiance for creative thinking.
- **White Noise / Fan**: Steady acoustic masking for deep reading and coding.
- **Coffee Shop**: Subtle background murmur that helps focus.

### Quick Distraction Scratchpad
Notice a random thought pop up while in deep focus (*"Remember to pay utility bill"* or *"Look up flight prices"*)? 
- Open the **Scratchpad** right inside the Focus Hub.
- Type it in and return immediately to your work without leaving the screen.
- Your scratchpad notes can be reviewed and converted into tasks later.

## 5. Goals, Projects & Milestones

AutoPlanner connects your immediate daily tasks to your big-picture dreams.

### Creating a Goal Hierarchy
1. Go to the **Goals** tab.
2. Tap **+ New Goal** (e.g., *"Run a Half Marathon"* or *"Launch Mobile App"*).
3. Set your target completion date and category.
4. Add **Milestones** or sub-projects (e.g., *"Run 5k"*, *"Run 10k"*, *"Run 15k"*).
5. When creating daily tasks, assign them to this goal. Every time you complete a task, your goal's progress ring advances!

## 9. AI Assistant Connection Modes

In **Settings > AI Assistant Connection**, you can choose how your smart scheduling is powered:

| Mode | Best For | Internet Needed? | Cost |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Google Gemini Key** | Recommended for personalized, state-of-the-art AI parsing & coaching | Yes | Free (Personal API Key) |
| **Offline Mode** | Maximum privacy, zero setup, battery saving, airplane mode | No | 100% Free |
| **Custom Server** | Self-hosted instances or private team gateways | Local/Private | Self-hosted |

### How to get a Free Google Gemini API Key:
1. Visit [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/).
2. Sign in with your Google account.
3. Click **"Get API key"** and copy your key.
4. Open AutoPlanner, go to **Settings > Gemini API Key**, paste your key, and tap **Save Key**.
5. Your key is stored securely inside your device's encrypted hardware vault and is never sent to any third-party server.

## 10. Data Privacy, Security & Backups

Your data belongs strictly to you.

- **On-Device Storage**: All tasks, notes, goals, and focus logs are stored locally on your device in an encrypted database (AES-256).
- **No Data Selling**: We do not collect, track, or sell your personal information or calendar contents.
- **Exporting a Backup**:
  - Go to **Settings > Data & Storage > Export Backup File**.
  - Save your JSON backup file to Google Drive, iCloud, or your device.
- **Restoring a Backup**:
  - If you switch devices, tap **Import Backup File** in Settings to instantly restore all your tasks and history.

## 11. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### Do I need to pay for a subscription to use AutoPlanner?
No. AutoPlanner core features — including smart timeline scheduling, focus timer, offline smart suggestions, goals, and habits — are completely free to use.

#### Can I use AutoPlanner without an internet connection?
Yes! AutoPlanner was designed from the ground up to be fully functional offline. When disconnected, it uses built-in smart algorithms to pack your schedule and provides pre-loaded productivity coaching.

#### Why does my schedule shift automatically?
If you enable **Automatic Schedule Catch-Up**, the app will helpfully adjust remaining tasks forward when an earlier event runs late. If you prefer fixed times for certain items, simply tap the lock icon on those tasks.

#### How do I reset or clear my data?
Go to **Settings > Data & Storage > Reset App Data**. You will be prompted to confirm before any data is cleared.
